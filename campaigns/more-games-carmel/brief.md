# Brief — Angle 3: "more games isn't enough"

**Series:** Multi-Sport Foundations (Athletes Acceleration) — ad copy series #3.
**Location:** Indianapolis (eyebrow anchors `INDIANAPOLIS SPORT PARENTS`).
**Pain state (Topic 6):** "He just needs more game experience — playing more will make him better."
**Audience:** Parent, 38–45, loading up on more teams / seasons / playing time, expecting volume to produce development.

**Mechanism (the argument):** Games *measure* the athlete training built; they don't *build* one. A game is a test, not a teacher — it reveals what's already there, it doesn't add to it. So another season of games just re-runs the same ceiling. Speed and power get built on purpose in training, or not at all.

**Epiphany:** "A game is a test, not a teacher."

**Proof (matched to the claim — NO forced RCT):**
- The Athlete Analysis baseline + the visible-numbers mechanism (you see real sprint/jump numbers).
- Case study — "one dad": his son spent two years somewhere else; one week with us and the kid said it was the best he'd ever had, because for once something was being *built into* him instead of *measured out of* him.
- Credentials: Coach Graham Wilkerson, CSCS; trained NFL athletes Zack Martin, Ted Karras, Nick Martin.
- The guarantee (verbatim, brand-locked).
